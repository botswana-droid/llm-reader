LLM Reader

Read books and PDFs deeper, in three passes.

  1. Pass 1 — Read it yourself. The old-fashioned way. No AI. Just you and the chapter, marking what surprises or confuses you.
  2. Pass 2 — Highlight what matters. Select any passage to save it as a highlight. Highlights stay in the book and stack up in the Highlights tab.
  3. Pass 3 — Send it to Claude or ChatGPT. Copy any chapter, your highlights, or the whole book to the clipboard with a ready-to-go prompt. Paste into the Claude.ai or ChatGPT app.

Your library

    Books and highlights stay on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is uploaded anywhere — copy/paste is the handoff.
